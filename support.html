Kitty City support

Need help, or want to tell us about a problem? Email gardenr946@gmail.com. We reply within two days.

How do I play with a friend?

Choose Join a friend and tap the same four pictures on both devices. Everyone who enters the same four pictures is in the same town.

How do I make friends?

Tap another kitty. Both kitties say yes and they are friends. Friends can visit each other's houses and send postcards.

How do I block or report someone?

Tap the other kitty's name, then choose Block or Report. A blocked kitty disappears and cannot be added as a friend again. Grown-ups can also remove a friend on the Grown-ups screen.

Where is the Grown-ups screen?

Tap the gear, then Grown-ups. Answer the multiplication question to open it. It has the friends list.

Our game progress is gone. Can we get it back?

The game keeps its saves on the device. If a grown-up made a backup (gear, then Backup), open it again with the Restore button on the start screen. Clearing the browser's website data deletes the save, so make a backup first if you plan to.

Is it safe for children?

Nobody types messages to each other. Players can only meet by sharing a four-picture key, names are letters only and checked by a word filter, and friends are made only when both kitties say yes. There are no ads, purchases or accounts. Read the privacy policy.
